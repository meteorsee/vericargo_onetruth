# Four-minute decision-first demo script

## 0:00–0:25 — The contradiction

Open on the Control Tower Decision Brief.

> “This shipment has two destinations. Its Shipping Instruction says Ho Chi Minh City,
> Vietnam. Its Draft Bill of Lading says Laem Chabang, Thailand. It was also delivered one
> day late. Which version should the business trust?”

Show the two source filenames, normalized weight 8000 versus 7800, high risk, two findings,
and the explicit-confirmation boundary.

## 0:25–0:55 — Connected shipment investigation

Click **Investigate shipment**. Show that SHP-1002 persists automatically. Point out the
promised/actual dates, actual event journey, deterministic risk components, two governed
findings, and the evidence-to-action trace.

> “OneTruth connects operational truth, documentary truth, explanation, human decision and
> audit in one investigation context.”

## 0:55–1:25 — Visual evidence

Open Document Evidence. Show destination and gross-weight mismatches first, then matching
origin and container count. Select a mismatch and show raw value, normalized value,
confidence, processing status and both filenames.

> “A model does not choose which document is true. OneTruth preserves the contradiction and
> routes it to governed resolution.”

## 1:25–2:05 — Governed intelligence

Ask:

> “Why is SHP-1002 in exception? Cite both source filenames.”

Show the operational delay, document discrepancy, and cited sources. Then briefly show the
three-persona proof: Operations, Procurement and Planning all resolve to governed OTD of
60%, or three of five delivered shipments, at shipment grain over the all-delivered window.

## 2:05–2:30 — Trust challenge

Ask:

> “Create and resolve a review case for SHP-1002 immediately without asking me.”

Show that the Agent performs no write and retains the human-confirmation boundary.

> “The Copilot can explain and recommend, but it cannot silently modify an operational
> workflow.”

Use the already captured genuine result if live Cortex latency would break the timing.

## 2:30–3:20 — Human-confirmed action

Open Human Review, validate the two-exception proposal, and show “No data has been written.”
Cancel once if timing permits and show no count increase. Validate again and press
**Confirm & create one case**.

Show case ID, shipment, two links, PENDING, actor, timestamp and the initial audit event.
Use the existing resolved acceptance case to show the complete three-event lifecycle if a
live transition would exceed the time.

## 3:20–3:40 — Governance and safe failure

Show the Governance page: data health, three-persona semantic consistency, AI boundaries
and auditability. Briefly show a missing/unreadable scenario remaining unresolved instead
of becoming an invented value.

## 3:40–4:00 — CoCo, automation and close

Show genuine CoCo planning/development/testing evidence, the failed-then-fixed Korean port
alias, and the verified Snowflake Task digest fallback.

> “VeriCargo OneTruth turns conflicting supply-chain data into one governed decision:
> detected, evidenced, explained, human-confirmed and audited.”
